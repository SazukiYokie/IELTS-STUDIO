IELTS Guide: how the real test works

Practise exactly like the exam day. Timings and question counts below follow the official IELTS format (Academic).

Conversion tables differ slightly between test versions, so treat these as estimates. Your overall band is the average of the four skills, rounded to the nearest 0.5.

Writing is marked on 4 criteria

  • Task Achievement / Response: answer every part of the question
  • Coherence & Cohesion: clear paragraphs and linking
  • Lexical Resource: range and accuracy of vocabulary
  • Grammatical Range & Accuracy: variety of structures, few errors

Speaking is marked on 4 criteria

  • Fluency & Coherence: speak at length without long pauses
  • Lexical Resource: flexible, precise vocabulary
  • Grammatical Range & Accuracy
  • Pronunciation: clear, natural stress and intonation

How to use this site

  1. Start with a single section / passage to learn the question types, then move to a full test under timed conditions.
  2. In Writing and Speaking, press Generate to get an exam-style task, then ask the AI examiner to grade it. Read the feedback and rewrite.
  3. Check My Scores & Tests weekly: aim for steady progress toward your target band.

Dictation practice

Listen to one sentence at a time and type exactly what you hear. It trains your ear for small words and spelling, and shows you which words you missed. Capital letters and punctuation are not marked.

Examiner tips for Listening
  • Read the questions before the recording starts and underline key words.
  • Answers follow the order of the recording. If you lose one, move on.
  • Listen for corrections ("No, sorry, I meant..."): the final version is the answer.
  • Check spelling, singular/plural and the word limit. Write numbers as digits.

Full Reading Test

3 passages, 40 questions, 60 minutes. True/False/Not Given, matching, multiple choice and completion. Difficulty rises each passage.

Practise one passage

About 13 questions in 20 minutes. Choose a difficulty.

Examiner tips for Reading
  • Skim the passage for the main idea first, then scan for key words from each question.
  • True/False/Not Given: False means the passage says the opposite; Not Given means it says nothing about it.
  • For completion questions use words exactly as written in the passage and respect the word limit.
  • Spend about 20 minutes per passage. Never leave an answer blank.
